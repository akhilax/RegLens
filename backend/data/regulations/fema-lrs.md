> DEMO SUMMARY — paraphrased for educational use; verify against the official source.

## Para 1 — Remittance limit under LRS {#lrs-limit}
Under the Liberalised Remittance Scheme, Authorised Dealer banks may freely allow remittances by resident individuals up to USD 250,000 per financial year (April–March) for any permitted current or capital account transaction or a combination of both. Remittances beyond this limit require prior approval of the Reserve Bank.

## Para 2 — Permitted purposes {#lrs-purposes}
Permitted purposes include private visits, gifts and donations, going abroad for employment or studies, medical treatment, maintenance of close relatives abroad, and investment in overseas securities and property. Each remittance carries a purpose code (for example S0305 for education-related travel and S0306 for other travel — verify the current purpose code list).

## Para 3 — Monitoring by AD banks {#lrs-monitoring}
The Authorised Dealer bank must obtain the remitter's PAN and a declaration of remittances already made in the financial year, and must ensure that the cumulative remittances of the individual across the financial year do not exceed the LRS limit. Remittances from a high-risk jurisdiction perspective remain subject to KYC and AML requirements.

## Para 4 — Tax Collected at Source {#lrs-tcs}
Tax may be collected at source by the remitting bank on LRS remittances above the prescribed annual threshold, at rates that vary by purpose (verify current thresholds and rates, which were revised by recent Finance Acts).
