> DEMO SUMMARY — paraphrased for educational use; verify against the official source.
> The Income-tax Act, 2025 replaced the Income-tax Act, 1961 with effect from 1 April 2026 and renumbered its sections. Old references are given below; new-Act references marked "verify" must be checked against the official text.

## Cash receipt restriction — 1961 Act s.269ST (2025 Act: verify) {#cash-receipt-2l}
No person shall receive an amount of two lakh rupees or more in cash in aggregate from a person in a day, in respect of a single transaction, or in respect of transactions relating to one event or occasion from a person. Receipts by banks, post offices and certain government entities are excluded. The penalty on the recipient is a sum equal to the amount received (1961 Act s.271DA; 2025 Act: verify). Banks monitoring business accounts should alert on cash receipts that indicate a breach by the account holder.

## PAN for cash deposits above ₹50,000 — 1962 Rules r.114B (new Rules: verify) {#pan-cash-50k}
A Permanent Account Number must be quoted for a cash deposit aggregating more than fifty thousand rupees during any one day with a banking company or co-operative bank. A person without a PAN must furnish a declaration in Form 60. Depositing just below this limit on multiple days may indicate an attempt to avoid identification.

## TDS on large cash withdrawals — 1961 Act s.194N (2025 Act: verify) {#tds-cash-withdrawal}
A bank must deduct tax at source on cash withdrawals by a person exceeding one crore rupees in aggregate during a financial year from one or more accounts maintained with it. Lower thresholds and higher rates apply to persons who have not filed returns for the prescribed preceding years (verify current limits).

## Statement of Financial Transactions — 1961 Act s.285BA (2025 Act: verify) {#sft}
Banks must furnish a Statement of Financial Transactions for specified transactions, including cash deposits aggregating ten lakh rupees or more in a financial year in one or more accounts (other than a current account and time deposit) of a person, and cash deposits or withdrawals aggregating fifty lakh rupees or more in current accounts (verify current thresholds and form references).
