## Section 2(1)(wa) — Reporting entity {#sec2-1-wa}
A reporting entity means a banking company, financial institution, intermediary or a person carrying on a designated business or profession. Banks are reporting entities and are therefore bound by the record-keeping and reporting obligations of the Act and the Rules made under it.

## Section 3 — Offence of money-laundering {#sec3}
Whosoever directly or indirectly attempts to indulge, knowingly assists, knowingly is a party to, or is actually involved in any process or activity connected with the proceeds of crime, including its concealment, possession, acquisition or use, and projecting or claiming it as untainted property, is guilty of the offence of money-laundering.

## Section 12 — Obligations of reporting entities {#sec12}
Every reporting entity must maintain a record of all transactions, including information that permits reconstruction of individual transactions; furnish to the Director, FIU-IND, information relating to such transactions within the prescribed time; verify the identity of its clients and beneficial owners; and maintain records of documents evidencing identity. Records must be kept for five years after the transaction or the end of the business relationship.

## Section 12A — Access to information {#sec12a}
The Director may call for from any reporting entity any of the records referred to in Section 12 and any additional information as considered necessary. Every reporting entity must maintain the confidentiality of any information it provides, and must not disclose ("tip off") to the client that a report has been or will be made.

## Section 13 — Powers of Director to impose fine {#sec13}
If the Director finds that a reporting entity or its officers have failed to comply with the obligations under the Chapter, the Director may issue a warning, direct compliance, require reports on measures taken, or impose a monetary penalty for each failure (the statute prescribes a per-failure range; verify current amounts).

## Section 14 — No civil proceedings against reporting entities {#sec14}
Save as otherwise provided in the Act, reporting entities, their directors and employees are protected from civil or criminal proceedings for furnishing information in good faith under Section 12.
